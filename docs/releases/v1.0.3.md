# CrimsonHue 1.0.3

Reduces rapid local-light fluctuations during movement with two-stage smoothing
after spatial mapping. The selected response still reaches 63.2% of a steady
change in the same time. Quick, Balanced, Calm, saved Custom values, source
weights and room calibration remain unchanged.

The captured quiet-corridor replay has about 66% less rapid output variation at
30 Hz. Two later bursts in the owner's separate lightning-ball recording retain
pronounced calculated output and nearly the same isolated blue-light time
integrals. Short individual pulses can be softer or peak slightly later; these
are recorded-input/offline results, not physical lamp acceptance or spell detection.

Use **CDT 2.2.7** for the complete update. Its independent cache-admission fix
prevents historical light positions from blocking new visibility queries during
rapidly changing effects. It keeps the native ray budget and fresh-clear rules;
Hue still uses the neutral raw API, never treats unknown as clear and performs
no game-memory reading or occlusion test.

Ambient retains its independent response. Losing the whole local feed immediately
clears output/history; master and channel brightness limits still apply immediately.
There are no new controls, schema requirements or game-build allowlists.

Local checks: 131 Core regressions, exact packaged hidden-controller smoke,
expanded/ZIP validation and actual-Core offline replays. Full measurements and
limits: [motion-response validation](../MOTION-RESPONSE-20261103.md).

Close the game and install **CrimsonHue-1.0.3-asi-win-x64.zip** and the complete
CDT 2.2.7 package yourself through your mod manager. .NET 8 Desktop Runtime x64
remains required separately. Installation is exclusively the owner's responsibility.
